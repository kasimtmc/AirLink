Cloud drives on the computer: OneDrive, Dropbox and Google Drive in AirLink's window, next to your devices.

**New: cloud drives** (Linux and Windows)

- Connect your accounts in **Settings → Cloud drives**. You sign in in your browser, and each account gets its own tab.
- Browse folders and search the whole drive.
- Download files, or upload files and whole folders with **Yükle** (Upload) or by dragging them onto the tab. Large files go up in parts, with progress, and you can cancel them.
- Make new folders, rename, and delete. Deleted files go to the drive's recycle bin.
- Send a file from the drive straight to a paired device, such as your phone.
- See how much of the drive's space is used.
- Google's own documents download as Office files: Docs as .docx, Sheets as .xlsx, Slides as .pptx.
- Show files as a list or a grid, with thumbnails of photos and videos and an icon for each file type. A finished download or upload leaves after a few seconds and stays in the account's **History** tab.
- Your sign-in is kept in the system keyring: KWallet or GNOME Keyring on Linux, Credential Manager on Windows. Files go between your computer and the provider directly; there is no AirLink server in between.

**Also new**

- **Updates:** AirLink looks for a new version on GitHub and updates itself from **Settings → General**: the AppImage, the .deb (it asks for your password), the tar.gz folder with its menus and KDE panel widget, the Windows installer and the Android APK. The Flatpak opens in your software center. Turn off the automatic check there too.
- **Clipboard in the background:** on Linux (Wayland), copies are sent to trusted devices with AirLink's window closed to the tray, and text from them arrives there too. On Android, with advanced mode set up, copies are sent with the app in the background or closed.
- **Phone screen and apps:** the phone's screen opens in a window of its own with Back, Home and Recents. Right-click a phone app in **Apps** to add it to the app menu or the desktop: the shortcut opens it in its own window, without AirLink's.
- **Tray panel:** notifications, messages, calls, contacts and each cloud drive are tabs under your devices; the panel has rounded corners, and its icon opens and closes it.
- **Files tab:** search, a list or a grid, photo thumbnails and file type icons.
- **Rename devices:** the pencil by a paired device's name gives it a name of your own on this device.
- **"Send with AirLink"** from the file manager shows the device choice even with AirLink's window closed to the tray.
- **English:** AirLink speaks English as well as Turkish. It follows the system's language; choose one in **Settings → General → Language** (**Ayarlar → Genel → Dil**), and the window switches at once.
- **Cancel a transfer:** a file transfer under way can be cancelled with its **X**, on either device. Both sides see it as cancelled, and nothing half-received is kept.
- **Windows notifications:** files and clipboard text that arrive while AirLink's window isn't in front show a notification on Windows too.

**Good to know**

- **Google Drive:** Google hasn't verified AirLink yet. When you connect, Google shows a "Google hasn't verified this app" screen; choose **Continue** (under **Advanced** if needed). Until Google verifies the app, at most 100 people can connect a Google account.
- **OneDrive with a work or school account:** your organization may need to approve AirLink first. Personal Microsoft accounts connect directly.
- **Android:** the phone has no drive tabs; the providers' own apps cover it there.
