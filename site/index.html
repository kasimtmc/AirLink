Your Linux, Windows and Android devices, linked on your own network.

No account and no cloud of ours: devices find each other on your network, pair once with a six-digit code, and talk to each other directly over an encrypted connection.

Download

What it does

  • Files and folders between paired devices: drag and drop, the share sheet, or the file manager's menu.
  • Clipboard shared between devices, with a history.
  • Phone notifications, SMS and calls on the computer: reply, write messages, answer calls.
  • The phone's screen and apps in windows on the computer, and phone audio over Bluetooth.
  • Battery and find my phone.
  • Cloud drives on the computer: OneDrive, Dropbox and Google Drive, each in its own tab. Browse, search, download, upload, organize, and send a file straight to your phone.

Cloud drives

When you connect a cloud drive, you sign in to its provider in your browser and allow AirLink to reach your files there. AirLink then talks to the provider directly from your computer. Your files and your sign-in never pass through any server of AirLink's: there is none. See the privacy policy for exactly what is used and where it is kept.
